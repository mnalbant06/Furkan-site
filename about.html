About & CV

I am a science-focused student at Sivas Cumhuriyet University Foundation Science High School in Türkiye. Mathematics and the natural sciences are the subjects I enjoy most, and I hold both a merit-based scholarship and an athlete scholarship.

My main interest is polymer-based biomedical materials. Through a TÜBİTAK 2204-A research project I have worked hands-on with UV polymerisation, antimicrobial coatings and microbiological testing. I want to study Materials Science, Biomedical Engineering or Polymer Engineering, and to build sustainable technologies that help people and the environment.

Basketball has taught me discipline and teamwork, and wildlife photography has taught me patience and careful observation. Both shape how I approach research.
