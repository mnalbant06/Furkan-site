Beyond the Lab

Sport, nature and creative work shape how I think and work: discipline and teamwork from basketball, patience and observation from wildlife photography.

Sport

Basketball

I have played as a licensed player since the age of eight, as a point guard. I came up through the youth system of Cumhuriyet University Foundation College Basketball Club (CVK) and train at the Kaan Karasu Basketball School.

Basketball taught me to set goals, to keep working when results are slow, and to make quick decisions as part of a team. I bring the same habits to my research.

Nature

Wildlife photography and bird watching

I have photographed birds and other wildlife since 2022 with a Canon 60D and a Canon EF 400mm f/5.6 lens. Watching birds teaches me patience and careful observation, which matter just as much in the laboratory.

I share my photographs to raise awareness of nature and bird conservation.

Making

3D design and theatre

I measure real objects with a micrometre caliper, turn the measurements into drawings, build STL files in beginner 3D software, and print them on a 3D printer. I enjoy turning an idea into a physical object.

I also take part in theatre groups and drama projects, which help me communicate clearly and work with other people.
